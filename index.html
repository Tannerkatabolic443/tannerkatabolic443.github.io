🤖 ap-automation-agent - Automate your accounts payable workflow today

Download Software

This application handles accounts payable tasks without manual data entry. It uses artificial intelligence to read invoices, match them against your records, and sync data with your accounting software. The application also formats Word documents to meet your business standards.

✨ Key Features

  • Reads invoice data using GPT-4o technology.
  • Checks invoices against purchase orders and receipts automatically.
  • Routes approvals to the correct people.
  • Syncs finalized data with your existing ERP system.
  • Standardizes DOCX files for consistent documentation.

💻 System Requirements

Your computer needs the following specifications to run this software:

  • Operating System: Windows 10 or Windows 11.
  • Processor: 1.6 GHz or faster.
  • Memory: 4 GB of RAM.
  • Storage: 200 MB of available disk space.
  • Network: Active internet connection for cloud processing.

📥 How to Download and Install

Follow these steps to set up the application on your computer:

  1. Visit the release page to download the latest version.
  2. Locate the Windows installer file, which typically ends in .exe.
  3. Double-click the file to start the installation wizard.
  4. Follow the prompts on your screen to complete the setup process.
  5. Open the application from your desktop shortcut once installation finishes.

⚙️ Setting Up the Agent

Once you open the software, follow these steps to configure your workspace:

  • Log in with your provided credentials.
  • Point the application to the folder where you store your incoming invoices.
  • Enter your ERP connection details in the settings tab.
  • Define your preferred approval routing rules.
  • Click the Start button to begin automated processing.

🔒 Data Privacy

The application processes your invoices through a secure connection. Data remains encrypted during transfer. Local files stay on your machine unless you select them for cloud-based AI processing. We do not store your original documents on our servers after the task finishes.

🛠 Troubleshooting

If you encounter issues, verify your internet connection. Check that your PDF or DOCX files are clean and legible. If the software fails to sync with your ERP, check your API credentials in the settings menu. Reach out to your IT administrator if errors persist.
